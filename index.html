Welcome to SRThub

Fast, creator-first toolkit for subtitles, timestamps, and transliteration.

SRThub is an essential, fast toolkit designed for creators who work with subtitles, timestamps, and text daily. As a video editor, subtitle writer, researcher, or independent creator, you need tools that deliver results without the hassle of accounts, uploads, or convoluted workflows. SRThub meets this demand by operating entirely in your browser, ensuring your files remain on your machine and your data stays private. With SRThub, you can swiftly generate clean .srt files from raw text, create precise timestamped YouTube links and chapter data from existing subtitles, and transliterate text between English and Hindi with guaranteed safe output. Each tool is crafted to be simple, focused, and effective—no distractions, no unnecessary options. We prioritize your privacy: there are no saved uploads, no background tracking, and no hidden processing. What you paste, upload, or generate stays on your system. SRThub is built to respect your time and uphold your privacy. While many subtitle tools overcomplicate the process, SRThub takes a different route. It remains lightweight, offline-friendly, and purpose-driven, allowing you to accomplish your work efficiently. If you demand a tool that delivers performance—cleanly, quickly, and locally—choose SRThub. It’s built for you.
